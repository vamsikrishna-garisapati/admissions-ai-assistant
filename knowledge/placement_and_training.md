# Placement Support & Training Methodology

## Training Methodology
- **Hands-on Coding Practice:** 80% practical coding, 20% theory.
- **Daily Assignments & Weekly Assessments:** Regular coding tests and code reviews by industry mentors.
- **Real-World Capstone Projects:** Production-ready applications built from scratch, reviewed on GitHub.
- **Mentorship:** Dedicated technical mentors to assist in doubt-solving during lab hours.

## Placement Assistance
- **Dedicated Placement Cell:** Active corporate relations team scheduling on-campus and off-campus drives.
- **Resume Building:** Professional technical resume preparation and LinkedIn profile optimization.
- **Mock Interviews:** 1-on-1 technical mock interviews, system design discussions, and HR behavioral preparation.
- **Aptitude & Soft Skills:** Dedicated modules on communication, problem-solving, and interview etiquette.
- **Company Drives:** Regular recruitment drives with hiring partners including IT service firms, product startups, and tech consultancies.

## Policy Note on Placements
- We provide comprehensive placement support, interview preparation, and job drive access to eligible students who complete their projects and meet attendance benchmarks.
- We do not offer or guarantee fake job promises; job placement depends on student dedication, project mastery, and interview performance.
