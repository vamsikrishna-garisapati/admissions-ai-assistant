# Frequently Asked Questions (Admissions FAQs)

### Q1: I am from a non-IT / mechanical / civil background. Can I join?
Yes! Our foundation modules start with core logic and computational basics before moving into advanced frameworks. Many of our successful students come from non-IT backgrounds.

### Q2: What is the course duration?
Standard full stack courses run between 4 to 6 months depending on whether you choose standard weekday or intensive fast-track batches.

### Q3: Do you offer online or offline classes?
Both options are available. You can attend offline classroom sessions at our campus or join interactive online live batches from anywhere.

### Q4: Will I receive placement assistance?
Yes, students who maintain 85%+ attendance and complete their capstone projects receive dedicated placement assistance, mock interviews, and drive referrals.

### Q5: How do I schedule a direct counselling call?
You can request a callback right here in the chatbot by sharing your name and contact number, and our admissions team will call you at your preferred time.
