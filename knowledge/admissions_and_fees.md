# Admissions, Fees & Batches

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Educational Background:** Any graduate or diploma holder (B.Tech, BE, MCA, BCA, BSc, B.Com with technical aptitude).
- **Passout Years Eligible:** Recent graduates and candidates with educational or career gaps.
- **Prior Coding Experience:** No prior programming experience required; training starts from fundamental logic building and problem-solving.

## Admission Process
1. **Initial Enquiry & Career Guidance:** Prospective student interacts with career counselor (or AI assistant) to identify suitable course.
2. **Syllabus & Demo Session:** Opportunity to attend a demo session or review detailed curriculum.
3. **Registration & Enrolment:** Complete enrolment form, submit qualification details, and select batch timing.
4. **Batch Allocation:** Receive orientation schedule and learning materials.

## Course Fees & Payment Options
- **Standard Fee Structure:** Contact admissions for the current promotional fee and scholarship eligibility.
- **Installment Facilities:** Flexible monthly installment / EMI options available through financial education partners.
- **Refund Policy:** Clear, upfront terms shared prior to enrolment during counseling.

## Upcoming Batches
- **Frequency:** New weekday and weekend batches start every 2 to 3 weeks.
- **Batch Timings:**
  - Morning Batch: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  - Afternoon / Lab Batch: 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  - Evening / Working Professional: Flexible schedules available.
